Personal Finance Calculators

The calculators here cover the money decisions most people run into repeatedly over a lifetime: loans, mortgages, taxes, savings, and retirement. If a decision involves your paycheck, your home, or your long-term savings, it's probably in this section.

Popular
🏦Loan / EMI→
Enter the amount, rate, and term to see your exact monthly payment. Useful before signing any loan, so you know the real cost — not just the headline rate.
✅Loan Eligibility→
See what loan amount you may qualify for based on income, debts, and credit profile. Check this before applying, so you don't waste a hard credit pull on a loan you won't get.
📊Debt-to-Income Ratio→
Calculates your debt-to-income ratio and checks it against typical lender thresholds. Lenders use this number to decide approval — know it before they do.
⏩Loan Prepayment→
Shows the time and interest you'd save by paying extra toward your loan each month. Useful for deciding whether prepaying beats investing that extra cash instead.
🔀Refinance Break-Even→
Works out how many months it takes to recoup refinancing closing costs. Answers the real question: will refinancing actually save you money before you move or sell?
📋Loan Comparison→
Compare up to three loan offers side by side on payment, total interest, and cost. Makes shopping multiple lenders simple instead of juggling separate quotes.Popular
🚗Auto Loan Calculator→
Car payment calculator with trade-in value, sales tax, and new-vs-used rates. Know your monthly payment and total cost before you're at the dealership finance desk.Popular
🧾Tax Estimator→
Estimate your US federal tax, effective rate, and take-home pay from your income. Helps with paycheck planning, freelance quarterly estimates, or job-offer comparisons.Popular
💵Salary Calculator→
Convert pay between hourly, daily, monthly, and yearly instantly. Handy when comparing job offers quoted in different formats, or negotiating a rate.Popular
🏠Mortgage→
Full monthly payment including taxes, insurance, and PMI — not just principal and interest. Gives you the true number to compare against your budget before house-hunting.
🏡HELOC Calculator→
Estimates your borrowing power against home equity, plus draw and repayment schedules. Useful for planning a renovation or big expense funded by your home's equity.
🏘️Rent vs Buy Calculator→
Projects your net worth over time under renting vs buying, and finds the break-even year. Removes the guesswork from the biggest financial decision most people make.
⚖️Mortgage Comparison→
Compare up to three mortgage offers at once on rate, payment, and total cost. Built for when you have multiple lender quotes and need to see them side by side.Popular
🏛️Savings Estimator→
Projects compound growth on your savings and shows how long it takes to double. Good for setting realistic expectations before you pick a savings account or CD.
🎈Inflation Calculator→
Shows what a sum of money was, is, or will be worth after inflation. Useful for translating an old salary, price, or goal into today's purchasing power.
🏦Provident Fund Calculator→
Projects EPF/PF maturity value from your contributions, employer match, and interest rate. Helps you sanity-check what you'll actually have at retirement.Popular
👴Retirement Planner→
Checks whether your current savings rate puts you on track for retirement, adjusted for inflation. The core number to know before assuming you're saving "enough."
📜Social Security Estimator→
Shows how claiming at 62 vs full retirement age vs 70 changes your monthly benefit. A few years' difference in timing can mean tens of thousands over your lifetime.Popular
🏦401(k) Calculator→
Projects your 401(k) balance at retirement, including employer match and contribution growth. Useful for checking if you're leaving free employer money on the table.
💎Millionaire Calculator→
Estimates the age you'll cross $1,000,000 at your current savings pace. A motivating, concrete milestone rather than a vague long-term goal.
🔥FIRE Calculator→
Calculates years to financial independence based on your savings rate and expenses. The core number behind any FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early) plan.
💼Net Worth Calculator→
Totals your assets and liabilities into one net worth number, with a liquidity and debt-to-asset breakdown. The single best snapshot of your overall financial position.
❄️Snowball vs Avalanche→
Compares the snowball and avalanche payoff strategies to show which clears your debt faster and cheaper. Removes the guesswork from picking a payoff order.Popular
💳Debt Payoff Planner→
Builds a custom payoff plan across all your debts and shows the interest it will save. Turns a pile of balances into one clear, ordered plan.
🎫Credit Card Payoff→
Compares paying the minimum vs a fixed payment on credit card debt, and the real cost difference. Minimum payments can take over a decade — this shows exactly how much that costs you.
🏘️Rental Yield→
Calculates gross and net rental yield against a property's purchase price. A quick way to screen whether a rental property is actually a good deal.
🏡Rental Property→
Projects cash flow, returns, and a 10-year outlook for a rental property investment. Goes beyond yield to show the full financial picture before you buy.
🎯Savings Goal Calculator→
Works out the monthly amount needed to hit a savings target by a specific date. Turns a vague goal like "save for a wedding" into an actual monthly number.Popular
🥧50/30/20 Budget Calculator→
Checks your actual spending against the 50/30/20 rule for needs, wants, and savings. A fast gut-check on whether your budget is balanced or overextended.
🛡️Emergency Fund Calculator→
Calculates how big your emergency fund should be and how long it'll take to build. Answers "how much is enough" instead of guessing at a round number.
💯Financial Health Score→
Scores your overall financial health out of 100 across savings, debt, and spending. One number to track progress instead of juggling a dozen separate metrics.
🛡️Life Insurance Needs→
Estimates the life insurance coverage your family would actually need, based on income and obligations. Avoids both under-insuring and overpaying for coverage you don't need.
🏥Health Insurance Cost→
Compare two health plans by their true total annual cost, not just the premium
