Investing & Valuation
15 calculators
Popular
📐CAGR Calculator→
Calculates the steady annual growth rate behind an investment's actual (bumpy) returns. The number that lets you fairly compare two very different-looking investments.
📅XIRR Calculator→
Calculates the true annualized return on cash flows that happen on irregular dates. What a normal return calculator gets wrong once you've added or withdrawn money mid-way.
💧Dividend Reinvestment→
Projects investment growth when dividends are automatically reinvested rather than taken as cash. Shows how much reinvesting compounds your returns over time.
📈Dividend Growth→
Projects your future dividend income growth and yield on cost for a dividend stock. Useful for anyone building toward dividend income as a goal.
🗂️ETF Growth→
Compares growth of popular ETFs like SPY, VTI, and QQQ side by side. A quick way to see how different index funds would have performed on the same money.
🥧Asset Allocation→
Suggests a stocks/bonds/cash mix based on your age and risk tolerance. A sanity check before you set (or reset) your portfolio allocation.
🏦Stock DCF Valuation→
Estimates what a stock is actually worth based on its projected future cash flows. Goes beyond the current price to ask whether a stock is over- or under-valued.
📏PEG Ratio→
Calculates the PEG ratio to judge if a stock's price is justified by its earnings growth, not just its P/E. Catches "expensive-looking" stocks that are actually cheap given their growth.
🏢EV/EBITDA→
Calculates enterprise value and shows what a peer company's multiple implies about a stock's worth. A valuation method that accounts for debt, unlike price-to-earnings alone.
📈SIP / Investment→
Projects a future lump sum from monthly SIP investing, including annual step-ups. Shows what consistent monthly investing actually adds up to over the years.
📊Dollar Cost Averaging→
Calculates shares bought and average cost under dollar-cost averaging vs a lump-sum investment. Helps decide whether investing gradually or all at once suits your situation.
📉SWP Calculator→
Shows how long a lump sum lasts under a fixed monthly withdrawal plan (SWP). Essential for anyone drawing down savings in retirement without running out early.
📉Stock P&L→
Calculates your real profit or loss on a stock trade after brokerage fees and taxes. The number your brokerage app doesn't always show you clearly.
⚡Options Profit & Breakeven→
Calculates breakeven price, max profit, and max loss for a call or put option. Know your actual risk and reward before placing an options trade.
🔀Stock Split→
Calculates your new share count and adjusted cost basis after a stock split. Keeps your portfolio records accurate after a split you didn't ask for.

Works out a product price using cost-plus, value-based, or competitor-based methods. Useful when you're not sure how to price something from scratch.

Shows how a new share offering affects existing shareholders' EPS and ownership value. Useful for evaluating the real cost of a company raising more equity.
📊Revenue Forecast→
Forecasts monthly recurring revenue under best-case, expected, and worst-case scenarios. Built for planning and investor conversations, not just a single guess.
🎯CAC & LTV:CAC→
Calculates customer acquisition cost and your LTV:CAC ratio to judge if growth is actually profitable. The metric that separates healthy growth from growth that's quietly losing money.
📈Break-Even Analysis→
Calculates how many units you need to sell before the business turns a profit. The first number any new product or business plan should be built around.
💵Cash Flow Analyzer→
Analyzes what's coming in, what's going out, and what's left over for your business. A clearer picture than your bank balance alone gives you.
